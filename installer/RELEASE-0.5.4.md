A joiner whose game restarted can hot-join again; a first-time player can load a shared save

- Bridge: a player whose game had restarted between attempts could never rejoin a running host. Their bridge came back under a new process id, the host's transport had frozen its member list at its last world change and silently refused the newcomer for the rest of the lobby's life -- while every packet the host sent waited for the newcomer's dead process, which stalled the host's sending for everybody else too (`pending=33` in the bridge log). The joiner sat in the loading gate with "0 peer(s) in". The world epoch is now the credential: whoever presents the current one is admitted (only the lobby hands it out), a member silent for 10 s -- not even a keepalive -- leaves the cohort and releases what waited for it, and a receiver whose sender has moved past what it waited for skips ahead instead of stalling for good. Every one of these, and every dropped datagram from another world, is now logged in `tpf2_bridge.log` (`[net] ...`), and the health line shows `members=` and `other-world=`.
- Lobby: after a resync, a player who joined LATER was placed in the lobby's original world nonce, which nobody was in any more, so their bridge and the host's dropped each other's packets -- and they were served the save from START GAME, a world the members had left at the resync. The lobby now advertises the resync epoch once the resync has completed and serves late joiners the resync snapshot; the members already in it take the new nonce as a rename, not a reset.
- Menu: a player who had never saved a single-player game could not load a shared save: the Steam account folder was found but its `local\save` folder did not exist yet, so placing the save failed with ERROR_PATH_NOT_FOUND and the game stayed on the title screen while everything up to that point (mods, transfer) reported success. The save folder is created when needed, the account is chosen by the best available folder, and the failure line names the destination.

Every player must update (the lobby version gate is exact) and the dedicated relay must run this version.

Validation: offline, the real-UDP transport tests (`test_net_epoch`, `test_net_multipeer` with 2/3/5/8 players, `test_net_packet_size`, and the new `test_net_restart`, which drives a restarted joiner, the eviction of its dead session, the old world still rejected, the skip to a sender's floor and re-admission), the lobby recovery and readiness tests including the new advertised-epoch rule; not yet in game with a second machine -- the case it fixes needs a remote player whose game restarts mid-session.
